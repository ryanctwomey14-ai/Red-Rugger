Target returns are targets, not promises

Any return figures shown on this website — including target annualized returns, target cash‑on‑cash returns and target equity multiples — are objectives we underwrite toward, not guarantees, projections of actual results, or commitments to pay. They are based on assumptions that may prove incorrect.

Actual results depend on property performance, occupancy, rent growth, renovation costs, financing terms, insurance costs, property taxes, the sale price achieved and general market conditions. Actual results may be materially lower than any target shown, distributions may be reduced or suspended, and you may lose some or all of the capital you invest. Past performance does not predict or guarantee future results.

Risk of loss

Investing in private real estate is speculative and involves a high degree of risk, including the risk of losing your entire investment. Risks include, without limitation: illiquidity and the absence of any secondary market; the use of leverage, which magnifies both gains and losses; changes in interest rates and operating expenses; tenant defaults and vacancy; adverse local, regional or national economic conditions; natural disasters and severe weather, which are a material consideration in Florida; insurance availability and cost; regulatory and zoning changes; construction and renovation cost overruns and delays; the possibility that a property cannot be refinanced or sold on acceptable terms; and reliance on Red Rugger's management.

Do not commit capital you may need access to before the end of the agreed term, and only participate if you can bear a complete loss.
