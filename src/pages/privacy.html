Red Rugger LLC ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information collected through redruggerllc.com.

The short version

  • We collect only what you choose to send us through a form, plus basic anonymous analytics.
  • We never sell, rent or trade your personal information.
  • We use your information to reply to you and to provide the service you asked about — nothing else.
  • You can ask us to delete your information at any time and we will.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you complete a form we collect the details you enter — typically your name, email address, telephone number, property or postal address, and whatever you write in the message field. Investor enquiry forms additionally collect the indicative amount you are considering and your timeline.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting and analytics tools may record your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and the pages you visit. This is used in aggregate to understand which pages are useful.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and this website is not directed at children.

How we use it

To respond to your enquiry; to provide the investment, lending, brokerage or property purchase service you asked about; to evaluate a property or a loan request; to send you information about opportunities where you have asked to receive it; to comply with legal, regulatory and anti‑money‑laundering obligations; and to improve the website.

If you enquire about investing, we keep a record of our communications with you so we can pick up where we left off and meet our record‑keeping obligations.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with: service providers who operate the website, email, CRM and form handling on our behalf, under confidentiality obligations; lenders, title companies, attorneys, accountants and transaction parties where necessary to progress a transaction you have asked us to pursue; and authorities where we are legally required to do so.

Confidentiality

If you contact us about selling a property, your situation is treated as confidential. We will not disclose that you contacted us, discuss your circumstances with neighbours, family or third parties, or publicise the property without your permission.

Cookies

We use only the cookies necessary to operate the site and, where enabled, basic analytics cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will continue to work.

Data retention and security

We keep personal information for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected and to meet legal and regulatory record‑keeping requirements, then delete it. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you may unsubscribe from any communication at any time using the link in the message or by emailing us.
